Questions

How do I request an appointment?

Tap Book an Appointment. WhatsApp opens with a ready message; review it and send it. The clinic then discusses dates, times and availability with you and confirms manually.

Is my appointment confirmed when I send the message?

No. Sending a message is an enquiry. The appointment is confirmed only when the clinic replies and agrees a time.

How do I find treatment pricing?

Use Enquire for Pricing on any service. It opens WhatsApp with a pricing question for that service.
